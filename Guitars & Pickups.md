## 1. Music Man Majesty 6-String (2019 Kinetic Blue)
* **Scale Length:** 25.5" scale, neck-through construction (Honduran Mahogany with Flame Maple shield).
* **Tuning:** E-Standard (Custom floating Music Man Piezo tremolo factory-balanced for `.010 – .046` string set).
* **Pickups:** **DiMarzio Rainmaker** (Neck) & **DiMarzio Dreamcatcher** (Bridge) + **Music Man Custom Piezo** saddle system.
* **Coil-Split Matrix:** Push-push **Magnetic Tone pot** activates coil splitting **only** when the 3-way magnetic toggle is in the **center position** (engaging the **inner coils of both pickups in parallel** for John Petrucci's signature glassy, chimey clean tone). In the neck-only or bridge-only toggle positions, the push-push tone pot does not split the individual humbuckers.
* **Gain Boost:** Push-push **Magnetic Volume pot** engages an adjustable up to **+20dB active analog gain boost** (powered by 3x AA batteries).
* **Piezo System & Acoustic Processing (`TC Electronic BodyRez` Nuance):**
  * The Majesty features a dual-output jack layout (**Mono/Stereo Magnetics** + **Dedicated Piezo/Mixed**) and an onboard active piezo preamp with rear-cavity bass, treble, and mix trimpots.
  * **Magnetic Output:** **Never** run the magnetic humbuckers through the [[Hardware & Routing#1. Front-of-Amp Pedals (Input Chain) & Drive Gain Staging|TC Electronic BodyRez]]. It applies an acoustic body-resonance filter that ruins electric high-gain and clean magnetic tones.
  * **Dedicated Piezo Output:** Because the Majesty's bridge uses under-saddle piezo crystals, raw direct piezo signal can still exhibit high-frequency piezo "quack." Running the dedicated **Piezo output -> TC Electronic BodyRez (set to 10:00–12:00)** -> clean amp channel or PA/interface applies phase-aligned FIR filters and subtle multi-band compression that restore natural wood warmth and hollow-body air. (Only bypass BodyRez if you are already loading a dedicated third-party Acoustic Guitar IR in a DAW or IR loader).

### Voicing Breakdown: Dreamcatcher vs. Rainmaker

**Bridge: DiMarzio Dreamcatcher (Ceramic)**
* **Sound Profile:** "The Sing." Open, highly dynamic, and vocal. Features a rounder, sweeter top end compared to traditional aggressive ceramics, letting the neck-through wood resonance cut through without harshness.
* **EQ Curve:** Boosted upper-midrange for immediate harmonic sensitivity (effortless pinch harmonics) paired with strong low-mids that prevent brittle highs or flubby lows.
* **Compression:** Low to moderate natural compression. Extremely responsive to right-hand pick velocity and volume knob rolloff.
* **Best Used For:** Modern progressive metal (Dream Theater *Distance Over Time* / *A View from the Top of the World*), expressive classical/orchestral dynamics, and lush high-gain lead work.

**Neck: DiMarzio Rainmaker (Alnico 5)**
* **Sound Profile:** Glassy, articulate, and exceptionally open. Avoids the boomy, muddy low-end typical of vintage neck humbuckers.
* **EQ Curve:** Balanced with a tailored dip in the lower-mids to maintain string-to-string clarity during high-speed sweeping or legato runs, plus a sweet upper-mid vocal peak.
* **Compression:** Very low natural compression; prioritizes breathy, expressive lead articulation and piano-like clean lows.
* **Best Used For:** Fluid, liquid solos (Joe Satriani / John Petrucci style), complex extended chord voicings, and crystal-clear clean passages.

### Voicing Breakdown: Voice 1 vs. Voice 2 vs. Voice 3

**Voice 1: Modern Active (Both Volume & Tone Knobs Pushed Down)**
* **Sound Profile:** Aggressive, percussive, and heavily compressed. The modern metal active benchmark (refined EMG 81-7 / 707 style without the sterile mid-plasticity).
* **EQ Curve:** Massive, surgical low-end with slightly scooped lower-mids and a sharp boost in upper-mids/presence. Pick attack hits like shattering glass; low-B palm mutes sound explosive and instantaneous.
* **Compression:** High natural compression. Acts like a brick-wall limiter, evening out alternate picking and sweep runs across all 7 strings.
* **Best Used For:** Metalcore (Trivium), modern technical metal, tight djent rhythm, and surgical Metallica-style thrash crunch.

**Voice 2: Passive / Organic (Tone Knobs Pulled Up, Volume Knobs Down)**
* **Sound Profile:** Warm, vintage-voiced, and highly dynamic. Mimics hot, overwound passive humbuckers (Seymour Duncan JB/Custom/Pegasus on bridge; warm PAF on neck).
* **EQ Curve:** Pronounced, throaty midrange growl. Highs are rounder with zero active "fizz." Low-end blooms naturally rather than clamping down.
* **Compression:** Low compression. Highly sensitive to right-hand attack; cleans up smoothly when rolling back the guitar volume knob.
* **Best Used For:** Classic British heavy metal (Iron Maiden, Judas Priest, Ozzy), hard rock, doom/sludge metal, expressive bluesy leads, and organic edge-of-breakup tones.

**Voice 3: Single-Coil Split (Volume Knobs Pulled Up)**
* **Sound Profile:** True, noise-free single-coil chime with zero 60Hz hum. Delivers cutting, bell-like clarity for funk, pristine cleans (Dream Theater intros), or Ritchie Blackmore-style Stratocaster tones.

## 3. Schecter Reaper-6 FR
* **Scale Length:** 25.5" scale, Swamp Ash body with Burl top, Ultra-Thin 'C' Maple/Walnut multi-ply neck.
* **Bridge:** Floyd Rose Special / 1500 Series double-locking tremolo.
* **Tunings:** Alternate tunings workhorse (**Drop D**, **Drop C#**, **D Standard**, **Eb Standard**, **DADGAD**).
* **Pickups:** Upgraded with the **DiMarzio John Petrucci Set (DP227 LiquiFire Neck / DP228 Crunch Lab Bridge)**.

### Voicing Breakdown: Crunch Lab vs. LiquiFire

**Bridge: DiMarzio Crunch Lab (DP228 — Ceramic)**
* **Sound Profile:** "The Crunch." Aggressive, mid-heavy, and razor-sharp with massive low-mid punch and wide chord separation.
* **EQ Curve:** Huge low-mid authority to drive preamp tubes hard while keeping sub-bass tight. Tamed ultra-highs prevent harsh ice-pick frequencies on bright Swamp Ash/Maple guitars.
* **Compression:** High natural compression, providing a dense, focused "wall of sound" for staccato riffing and drop tunings.
* **Best Used For:** Heavy drop tunings (Tool, Linkin Park *"One Step Closer"*), 90s/00s progressive metal (*Images & Words*, *Awake*, *Train of Thought*, *Black Clouds & Silver Linings* era Dream Theater), and thrash rhythm.
* **Bar Orientation Calibration (Crucial Installation Detail):**
  * The Crunch Lab has an asymmetrical appearance: one coil has individual adjustable pole pieces, and the other has a **solid steel blade/bar**.
  * **Solid Bar Toward the Neck (John Petrucci's Official Spec):** Places the solid blade under a warmer, wider point of string excursion. Produces a **fatter, more open, vocal low-mid punch** with richer rhythm body and less brittle high-end. Recommended for progressive metal and drop-tuned Swamp Ash bodies like the Reaper-6.
  * **Solid Bar Toward the Bridge:** Places the solid blade right next to the saddles where string vibration is tightest and brightest. Produces a **sharper, brighter, more percussive pick click** with tighter sub-bass, preferred by some extreme downtuned djent players.

**Neck: DiMarzio LiquiFire (DP227 — Alnico 5)**
* **Sound Profile:** Fluid, vocal, and warm. Features a distinct tubular midrange character that prevents notes from blurring together during high-speed alternate picking or legato.
* **EQ Curve:** Focused mids with smooth, rounded top-end and singing sustain, avoiding the hollow scoop of vintage PAFs.
* **Compression:** Moderate compression that cushions pick attack and makes high-register notes sing like a violin.
* **Best Used For:** Fast alternate-picking passages, creamy lead solos (*"Under a Glass Moon"*, *"Glasgow Kiss"*), and warm clean arpeggios.

## 5. Hardware Mods & Electronics Engineering

### Schecter Reaper Stock Electronics, Pot Upgrades & Drop C# Setup
* **Stock Configuration:** Schecter Diamond Decimator passive humbuckers wired to **500kΩ potentiometers** (1x Master Volume, 1x Master Tone with Push-Pull coil tap, 3-way blade switch).
* **Upgrading to Crunch Lab / LiquiFire:**
  * The stock 500kΩ pots are 100% electrically compatible with the passive DiMarzio Petrucci set.
  * **Upgrade Recommendation:** Stock import pots have wide resistance tolerances (±20%) and abrupt taper curves. Upgrading to **CTS or Bourns 500kΩ Audio Taper pots** (with a `.022µF` Orange Drop or paper-in-oil tone capacitor) provides a smoother volume cleanup curve and superior mechanical feel.
* **Drop C# Mechanical Calibration (`.010–.052` Skinny Top Heavy Bottom):**
  * **Nut Slot Filing:** Moving from a `.046` to a `.052` 6th string (on hardtail or non-locking nuts) requires gently widening the 6th-string nut slot sides with a `.054"` nut file so the string sits on the bottom of the slot without binding. (On a Floyd Rose locking nut, check string retainer bar height).
  * **String Action (12th Fret):** **1.5mm (`1/16"`)** on the High E (1st string) and **2.0mm (`5/64"`)** on the Low C# (6th string) with `.010"` (0.25mm) neck relief at the 8th fret.
  * **Pickup Height (Crucial for Ceramic Magnets):** Depress the strings at the last fret and measure from the bottom of the string to the top of the pole piece: set **Crunch Lab Bridge to 2.5mm (`3/32"`)** on the bass side and **2.0mm** on the treble side; set **LiquiFire Neck to 3.0mm** bass / **2.5mm** treble. Setting ceramic pickups too close pulls the slack Drop C# string out of tune ("stratitis" magnetic warble) and chokes sustain.

### Floyd Rose & EVH D-Tuna Implementation
* **The Problem on Floating Trems:** The EVH D-Tuna mechanically drops the 6th string pitch from E to D by retracting a collar under the fine-tuner screw. On a free-floating Floyd Rose, dropping 6th-string tension relieves ~3–4 lbs of pull against the rear springs, causing the bridge baseplate to dip backward into the body cavity and pulling strings 1–5 sharp.
* **Mandatory Solution:** A bridge stabilization device—such as a **Tremol-No** (set to Dive-Only or Hardtail mode), an **ENGL/FU-Tone Brass Tremolo Stopper**, or a fitted wood/brass block between the sustain block and cavity wall—is strictly required so the bridge cannot tilt backward.
* **Physical Cavity Clearance Warning:** The Schecter Reaper-6 FR features a **recessed tremolo cavity**. The D-Tuna cylinder extends ~12mm behind the 6th-string saddle and will physically strike the top edge of the recessed guitar body unless the bridge is blocked for **dive-only operation** (preventing pull-ups) or a clearance channel is routed into the body finish.

### Matt Heafy 7-String Pickup Swap Pathways
The Epiphone Matt Heafy 7-String uses **Active Mount ("Soapbar" / EMG 707 footprint: `3.5" x 1.5"` / `88.9mm x 38.1mm`)** routings with no mounting rings.

* **Path A — Fishman Fluence 3-Voice Solderless Drop-Ins (Plug & Play):**
  * Uses the exact same **707 Soapbar housing** and rear **Quick-Connect Molex pin header** as the stock MKH pickups. Requires **zero soldering** and retains all 4 push/pull pots for Voices 1, 2, and 3:
    1. **Fishman Fluence Tosin Abasi 7-String Set:** Ultimate progressive metal/fusion versatility. Voice 1 is modern percussive active; Voice 2 is hi-fi passive crystal-clear cleans/mid-gain; Voice 3 is asymmetrical single-coil split that nails glassy Strat/Tele snap.
    2. **Fishman Fluence Javier Reyes 7-String Set (Alnico):** Warmer, lower-output, organic woody voicing designed for progressive rock, jazz-fusion, and expressive mid-gain.
    3. **Fishman Fluence Killswitch Engage (KSE) 7-String Set:** Tighter, mid-focused active/passive voicings with built-in tube-screamer-style low-cut tightness in Voice 1.
* **Path B — Passive Soapbar Conversion (Seymour Duncan Pegasus / Sentient 7 Active Mount):**
  * **Housing Selection:** You **must** order the **Active Mount (Soapbar)** version of the Seymour Duncan Pegasus (Bridge, Alnico 5 moderate-output prog metal pickup) and Sentient (Neck, articulate PAF/LiquiFire hybrid)—*not* the standard open-coil passive mount with triangular tabs.
  * **Full Electronics Gutting Required:** Active Fishman pickups use **25kΩ pots**. Connecting passive humbuckers to 25kΩ pots loads down the resonant peak and makes the guitar sound completely dead and muffled. You must replace:
    * **4x 500kΩ Long-Shaft Push/Pull Audio Taper Pots** (CTS or Bourns, required to clear the thick carved Les Paul Maple cap).
    * **2x `.022µF` Tone Capacitors** (wired from the tone pot lugs to ground).
    * **Bridge Ground Wire:** Active guitars often omit a string/bridge ground wire. You must verify or run a ground wire from the Tune-O-Matic bridge bushing into the control cavity and solder it to the back of a pot casing to prevent 60Hz hum.
  * **Seymour Duncan 4-Conductor Color Code & 3 Push/Pull Wiring Options:**
    * *Wire Colors:* **Black** = Hot Output | **Green + Bare** = Ground | **Red + White** = Series Link (soldered together for coil-splitting).
    * **Option 1 (Classic Individual Coil Splits):** Pull Neck Volume -> splits Sentient to inner/slug coil (Ground Red+White). Pull Bridge Volume -> splits Pegasus to inner/slug coil.
    * **Option 2 (Petrucci "Majesty" Inner-Coil Split):** Wire a single push/pull pot so that when the 3-way toggle is in the middle position, pulling the pot grounds the outer coils of both pickups simultaneously, yielding John Petrucci's signature glassy parallel inner-coil clean tone.
    * **Option 3 (Series / Parallel Humbucker Switching — Hum-Canceling):** Wire the Tone push/pull pots as **Series/Parallel switches** for each pickup. Pushed Down = standard Series Humbucker (thick, full output); Pulled Up = Parallel Humbucker (30% lower output, single-coil-like glassy snap and clarity, **100% hum-canceling**).
* **Path C — Passive Petrucci 7-String Sets (DiMarzio Crunch Lab 7 / LiquiFire 7 or Dreamcatcher 7 / Rainmaker 7):**
  * DiMarzio 7-string pickups only come in **Standard Passive Open-Coil** footprint (with long metal mounting ears), which are smaller than the Heafy's soapbar cavity (`88.9mm x 38.1mm`).
  * To install them cleanly without exposed wood gaps, you must use **7-String Soapbar-to-Passive Conversion Mounting Rings** (available from 3D-printed/custom CNC luthiery suppliers) plus the full **4x 500kΩ pot conversion** described in Path B.

## 6. String Gauges, Scales & Tuning Reference

### 7-String Gauge Selection & Physics (25.5" Scale)
* **The Low-B Tension Dilemma:** String tension is governed by $T = \frac{\mu \cdot (2 \cdot L \cdot f)^2}{386.4}$. On a 25.5" scale guitar tuned to B1 (61.74 Hz), a light `.054` or `.056` 7th string carries only ~13.5–14.5 lbs of tension (compared to ~17.5–19 lbs on strings 4–6). Hard right-hand pick attack causes the low B string to deflect widely and **pitch-drift sharp on the initial transient** before settling flat, creating muddy, out-of-tune low-end tracking and fret buzz.
* **Complete 7-String Set Gauge Breakdown (High 1st String to Low 7th String):**

| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Super Light 9-54** (EB 2623 / EXL120-7) | `.009` | `.011` | `.016` | `.024` | `.032` | `.042` | `.054` | **Not Recommended for Metal.** 7th string (`.054`) is far too loose (~13.5 lbs); causes pitch warble and fret buzz on palm mutes. |
