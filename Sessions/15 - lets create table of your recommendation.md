### **Strategic Rig Integration Notes**

* **The "Anti-Fizz" Rule:** For your **Mesa Mark VII**, keep the **Presence** control low (around 3-4). Presence in the Mark series introduces a high-frequency "fizz" that can mask the inter-voice clarity of complex chords. Use the **Treble** knob for gain definition and the **Graphic EQ** (2200Hz and 6600Hz sliders) for the "air."
* **The Marshall JVM "Secret":** Your **JVM410c** can get compressed and "mushy" on the OD1/OD2 Red modes. I recommend staying in **Orange modes** and using the **Boss SD-1** or **TS808** to provide the extra saturation. This keeps the "attack" of the note intact, which is crucial for your classical-inspired ear.
* **Spatial Clarity:** In the FX loop, use your **TC Triple Delay** on the "2290" setting (Digital). Analog or Tape delays often add a "darkness" that can muddy up your signal when playing dense, Petrucci-style riffs.

### **Technique Focus: "Piano-Style" Pick Attack**

When playing the **Classical Context** or **Rush** settings, try to move your picking hand closer to the bridge for "staccato" clarity (Beethovenian) and move toward the neck for a "flutey" legato (Rachmaninov). The **Dreamcatcher/Rainmaker** set in your Majesty is particularly sensitive to these positional changes.
