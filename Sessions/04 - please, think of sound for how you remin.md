## Target Tone: "How You Remind Me" (Nickelback) - Clean Intro

Chad Kroeger’s clean tone on this track is thick, slightly compressed, and utilizes subtle modulation to widen the stereo field. It is not a sparkly, thin funk clean; it has weight.

### Guitar Selection: Music Man Majesty

* **Pickup Position:** Center position (Neck + Bridge). If your Majesty has the coil-split engaged on the push/push tone pot, activate it. We want the bell-like clarity of the split coils to prevent the low-end from muddying the intricate chord voicings, while retaining the hum-canceling benefits.
* **Piezo Option:** If you want absolute authenticity, blend in about 30% of your Majesty's piezo acoustic output. Kroeger often blends acoustic and electric cleans in the studio.

### Amp Settings: Mesa Boogie Mark VII

We will use the Mesa for its superior headroom.

* **Channel:** 1
* **Mode:** Clean
* **Gain:** 4.5 (Just high enough to warm up the tubes without inducing preamp distortion)
* **Treble:** 6.5 (To accentuate the pick attack)
* **Mid:** 4.0 (Slightly scooped to leave room for the vocal frequencies)
* **Bass:** 5.0 (Keep it tight)
* **Presence:** 5.5
* **Master:** As loud as your environment permits to get the power tubes working.

### Pedal Mechanics & Settings

**1. Overdrives (TS808 & SD-1): BYPASSED**

* **The Mechanism:** The TS808 relies on *symmetrical diode clipping*, clipping both the top and bottom of the audio waveform equally, producing a smooth, compressed, odd-harmonic tone. The SD-1 uses *asymmetrical clipping*, clipping one side more than the other, generating even-order harmonics mimicking natural tube breakup.
* **Impact on Tone:** For this intro, **keep both pedals off**. Any diode clipping will squash the dynamic transients of your arpeggiated chords. You need maximum clean headroom.

**2. TC Electronic Spark Mini: OPTIONAL (Always On)**

* **Settings:** Level at unity (around 9 o'clock depending on your pickups).
* **Impact:** Use this strictly as a clean FET buffer to push a pristine, unclipped signal through your long cable runs into the front of the Mesa, ensuring no high-end frequency loss.

**3. TC Electronic Dreamscape: ENGAGED**

* **Settings:** Mode: Chorus 1. Speed: 3. Depth: 4. FX Level: 3.
* **Impact:** Kroeger uses a very subtle, slow chorus to give the clean tone width without making it sound like an 80s pop track. Keep the depth shallow.

**4. Marshall Delay: ENGAGED**

* **Settings:** Time: ~350ms. Repeats: 1-2. Level: Very low (barely audible).
* **Impact:** Provides a subtle slapback/room reflection to prevent the dry Mesa clean from sounding too sterile in an isolated room.

### Technique Execution

The intro utilizes open, ringing chord voicings (primarily variations of C minor and F suspended).

* **Right Hand:** Use hybrid picking (pick and fingers) rather than standard strumming. Pluck the root notes with your plectrum and use your middle and ring fingers to pull the higher strings simultaneously. This ensures all notes of the chord speak at the exact same volume and time, creating that piano-like, compressed studio sound.
* **Left Hand:** Let the open strings ring out for their full duration. Do not choke the chords early. The tension of the track relies on those dissonant suspended notes ringing over the moving bassline.
