## 🎸 Detailed Song Dial-In Recipe Cards

### 1. Jack White – *"You'll Never Fix Me"* & *"Thick as Thieves"* (*Frozen Charlotte*, 2026)
* **Sonic Goal:** Chaotic, unpolished 2026 Third Man Records garage-rock hostility, nasal 1960s catalog-guitar honk, and splatty square-wave fuzz—achieved by intentionally "sabotaging" your high-precision prog guitars with coil-splits, pre-gain band-pass EQ, and stacked overdrives.
* **Guitar Selection & Setup:**
  * [[Guitars & Pickups#1. Music Man Majesty 6-String (2019 Kinetic Blue)|Music Man Majesty 6-String]] (3-way toggle in **Middle Position** + **Tone Push-Push engaged** for parallel inner-coil split) or [[Guitars & Pickups#3. Schecter Reaper-6 FR|Schecter Reaper-6]] (Neck pickup split).
  * **Guitar Knobs:** Roll **Volume back to `6.5 – 7.0`** (starves modern pickup compression) and **Tone back to `6.0`** (shaves off hi-fi modern sheen).
* **Pre-Distortion "AM Radio" Lo-Fi Filter ([[EQ Guide#3. Boss EQ-200 Channel A & Channel B Master Curves|Boss EQ-200 Channel A]] — Placed *Before* Drives):**
  * **`30Hz` & `60Hz`:** `-15dB` | **`120Hz`:** `-10dB` | **`200Hz`:** `-5dB` | **`400Hz`:** `+5dB` *(boxy plastic/wood resonance)* | **`800Hz`:** **`+10dB`** *(core garage-rock bark)* | **`1.6kHz`:** `+5dB` | **`3.2kHz`:** `0dB` | **`6.4kHz`:** `-10dB` | **`12.8kHz`:** `-15dB`.
* **Track A — *"You'll Never Fix Me"* (Raucous Asymmetrical Garage Crunch):**
  * **Amplifier:** **Marshall JVM 410C — Crunch Channel (`Orange Mode`)** -> `Gain: 4.5` | `Bass: 4.0` | `Mid: 7.0` | `Treble: 6.0` | `Presence: 6.0`.
  * **Drive Pedal:** **Boss SD-1** -> `Level: 7.0` | `Tone: 6.0` | **`Drive: 8.0`** (pushes asymmetrical silicon clipping into jagged, uneven breakup).
* **Track B — *"Thick as Thieves"* (Stacked Square-Wave Velcro Fuzz):**
  * **Amplifier:** **Marshall JVM 410C — Crunch Channel (`Red Mode`)** -> `Gain: 4.0` | `Bass: 5.0` | `Mid: 7.0` | `Treble: 5.0` | `Presence: 6.0`.
  * **4-Pedal Front-End Stack (In Your Board's Exact Physical Order — All ON):**
    1. **Boss EQ-200 Ch A:** "AM Radio" mid-spike curve above *(chokes the guitar signal into a narrow 400Hz–1.6kHz band before hitting the clipping diodes)*.
    2. **Boss SD-1:** `Level: 7.0` | `Tone: 6.0` | **`Drive: 8.0`** *(asymmetrical silicon clipping feeding into...)*
    3. **Ibanez TS808:** `Level: 10` | `Tone: 5.0` | `Drive: 3.0` *(symmetrical clipping chops the peaks flat into a square wave)*
    4. **TC Spark Mini:** `+5dB` *(placed **last** after the drives so its linear headroom boosts the final voltage into the amp without being clamped by the SD-1/TS808 diodes)*.
* **Cabinet IR:** **`York Audio MRSH 412 M25` (`Mix 08` — Greenbacks)**.

### 2. Shinedown – *"Thick as Thieves"* (*Threat to Survival*)
* **Sonic Goal:** Wide, polished, radio-ready modern American hard rock rhythm with chest-hitting low-mids and crystal-clear chord voicings (distinct from Jack White's track of the same title).
* **Guitar Selection:** [[Guitars & Pickups#2. Epiphone Matt Heafy Les Paul Custom Origins 7-String|Epiphone Matt Heafy 7]] (Voice 1 Active or Voice 2 Passive) or [[Guitars & Pickups#3. Schecter Reaper-6 FR|Schecter Reaper-6]] (Drop D / E Standard).
* **Option A — Marshall JVM 410C (Studio Marshall Roar):**
  * **Channel:** **OD1 (`Orange Mode`)** -> `Gain: 5.0` | `Bass: 6.0` | `Mid: 5.5` | `Treble: 6.5` | `Presence: 6.0` | `Resonance: 6.5`.
  * **Front Boost:** **Ibanez TS808** (`Level: 9.0`, `Drive: 0`, `Tone: 5.5`).
* **Option B — Mesa/Boogie Mark VII (Modern Wall of Sound):**
  * **Channel:** **Channel 2 (`Mark VII Mode`)** or **Channel 3 (`Mark IV Mode`)** -> `Gain: 6.0` | `Bass: 3.0` | `Mid: 5.0` | `Treble: 6.5`.
  * **5-Band Graphic EQ:** Classic V (`80Hz: +60%`, `240Hz: +15%`, `750Hz: -65%`, `2200Hz: +50%`, `6600Hz: +35%`).
* **Cabinet IR:** **`York Audio MES 412 OS-V2` (`Mix 01`)**.

### 3. Dream Theater – Clean Arpeggios (*"Pull Me Under"*, *"Take The Time"*, *"Endless Sacrifice"*)
* **Sonic Goal:** Ultra-even, crystalline 1990s/2000s studio rack cleans (Roland JC-120 / Mesa TriAxis + System 6000 compression + TC 2290 chorus/delay) where every cross-picked string rings at identical volume.
* **Guitar Selection & Setup:**
  * **For E Standard (*"Pull Me Under"*):** [[Guitars & Pickups#1. Music Man Majesty 6-String (2019 Kinetic Blue)|Music Man Majesty 6-String]] — 3-way selector in **Center Position** + **Tone Push-Push UP** (parallel inner coils). Optional: Blend in **25–30% Piezo** via the [[Hardware & Routing#1. Front-of-Amp Pedals (Input Chain) & Drive Gain Staging|TC Electronic BodyRez]] (`11:00`).
  * **For D Standard (*"Endless Sacrifice"* Intro):** [[Guitars & Pickups#3. Schecter Reaper-6 FR|Schecter Reaper-6]] in **D Standard (`D-G-C-F-A-D`)** on the **LiquiFire Neck (Coil-Split pulled)** or middle position.
* **Amplifier Settings (Mesa/Boogie Mark VII):**
  * **Channel:** **Channel 1 (`Clean Mode`)** -> `Gain: 3.5 (9:30)` | `Bass: 4.5` | `Mid: 4.0` | `Treble: 6.5` | `Presence: 6.0`.
* **Pedal Dial-In:**
  * **TC Electronic HyperGravity (Front of Amp):** Mode: **`SPECTRA` (Multiband)** (or Petrucci *EverComp* TonePrint) -> `Sustain: 1:30–2:00` | **`Attack: 10:00`** *(lets initial pick "spank" pop through before clamping down)* | `Level: 12:30` | **`Blend: 2:00–3:30 (70–80% Wet)`**.
  * **Boss EQ-200 Channel B (FX Loop):** `60Hz: +2dB` | `120Hz: +2dB` | `200Hz: -2dB` | `400Hz: -3dB` | `3.2kHz: +3dB` | `6.4kHz: +4dB` | `12.8kHz: +3dB`.
  * **TC Electronic The Dreamscape (FX Loop):** **`CHORUS 1`** -> `Speed: 10:00` | `Depth: 2:00` | `FX Level: 1:00` | Toggle: `Normal`.
  * **TC Flashback Triple Delay (FX Loop):** `2290 Dynamic` or `Digital` mode at `450ms`, `Feedback: 20%`, `Mix: 10:30`.
* **Cabinet IR:** **Mark VII Factory Slot 6 (`2x12 Lone Star Open-Back`)** or **`York Audio MTCH 212 ESD-V2`**.

### 4. Dream Theater – High-Gain Rhythm & Liquid Lead (John Petrucci Signature)
* **Sonic Goal:** Surgical sub-bass tracking on complex 6- and 7-string riffs (*"Pull Me Under"*, *"Endless Sacrifice"*, *"The Glass Prison"*), transitioning seamlessly into thick, vocal, cello-like lead sustain (*"Under a Glass Moon"*, *"The Best of Times"*).
* **Guitar Selection:**
  * **6-String E-Std:** Majesty (Dreamcatcher Bridge for rhythm; Rainmaker Neck for fast liquid leads).
  * **6-String D-Std (*"Endless Sacrifice"*):** Schecter Reaper-6 (Crunch Lab Bridge / LiquiFire Neck).
  * **7-String B-Std (*"The Glass Prison"*, *"A Change of Seasons"*):** Epiphone Matt Heafy 7 (**Voice 2 Passive** with Tone rolled to `8/10`, or **Voice 1 Active** with `EQ-200 Ch A` sub-bass cut).
* **Amplifier Settings (Mesa/Boogie Mark VII):**
  * **Channel:** **Channel 3 (`Mark IIC+ Mode` for surgical *Images & Words* attack, or `Mark IV Mode` for thicker *Train of Thought* density)**.
  * **Pre-Gain Controls:** `Gain: 6.0 – 6.5` | **`Bass: 2.0 – 3.0`** *(Keep below 3.0!)* | `Mid: 4.5` | `Treble: 6.5 – 7.0` | `Presence: 5.5`.
* **Overdrive Boost (Ibanez TS808):**
  * `Level: 10.0` | `Drive: 0.0` (or `1.5` for leads) | `Tone: 5.0`.
* **Mesa Mark VII 5-Band Graphic EQ:**
  * **Rhythm Curve ("Classic Petrucci V"):** `80Hz: +70%` | `240Hz: +15%` | **`750Hz: -75%`** | `2200Hz: +55%` | `6600Hz: +35%`.
  * **Lead Curve ("Singing Mid-Balanced W"):** `80Hz: +35%` | `240Hz: +25%` | **`750Hz: -10% to 0%`** *(Do not deep-scoop leads!)* | `2200Hz: +40%` | `6600Hz: +10%`.
* **FX Loop Solo Boost ([[EQ Guide#3. Boss EQ-200 Channel A & Channel B Master Curves|Boss EQ-200 Channel B]]):**
  * Stomp CTL 2 to engage **Channel B**: `+2dB @ 400Hz`, `+3dB @ 800Hz`, **`+4dB @ 1.6kHz`**, `+2dB @ 3.2kHz`, `-3dB @ 12.8kHz`, Overall **`LEVEL: +3.5dB`** + **TC Triple Delay** (`Dual Dotted-Eighth + Quarter` at `375ms / 500ms`).
* **Cabinet IR:** **`York Audio MES 412 OS-V2` — `Mix JP` (Slot 8 for Rhythm) & `Mix 01` (Slot 4 for Leads)**.

### 5. Linkin Park – *"One Step Closer"* (*Hybrid Theory*)
* **Sonic Goal:** Brad Delson's massive, hyper-tight 2000s Drop C# wall-of-sound rhythm (originally recorded by blending a Mesa Dual Rectifier with a modded Marshall JCM800).
* **Guitar Selection & Tuning:**
  * [[Guitars & Pickups#3. Schecter Reaper-6 FR|Schecter Reaper-6]] calibrated in **Drop C# (`C# - G# - C# - F# - A# - D#`)** with **`.010 – .052` Skinny Top Heavy Bottom** strings on the **DiMarzio Crunch Lab** bridge pickup.
  * *(Why not play it on the 7-String in B-Standard?)* `"One Step Closer"` relies on rapid **open-string Drop C# pedal-point riffs (`0-0-8-7-0-3-2`)** and natural harmonics on the **5th and 7th frets** during the intro/verse—which are physically impossible to execute with the same open resonance by fretting the 2nd fret of a B-Standard 7-string!
* **Amplifier Settings (Mesa/Boogie Mark VII):**
  * **Channel:** **Channel 2 (`Mark VII Mode` — wider Recto-like low-mid authority)** or **Channel 3 (`Mark IV Mode`)**.
  * **Controls:** `Gain: 6.0` | `Bass: 2.5` | `Mid: 5.5` | `Treble: 6.5` | `Presence: 6.0`.
* **Front-End Conditioning:**
  * **Boss EQ-200 Channel A:** `30Hz: -8dB` | `60Hz: -4dB` | `120Hz: -3dB` | `200Hz: -2dB` | `800Hz: +2dB` | `1.6kHz: +2dB`.
  * **Ibanez TS808:** `Level: 10` | `Drive: 0` | `Tone: 5.5`.
* **Mesa 5-Band Graphic EQ:** `80Hz: +75%` | `240Hz: +10%` | `750Hz: -80%` | `2200Hz: +45%` | `6600Hz: +45%`.
* **Cabinet IR:** **`York Audio MES 412 OS-V2` (`Mix 07` or `Mix JP`)**.

### 6. Metallica – 1986 *"Master of Puppets"* vs. 1991 *Black Album* (*"Enter Sandman"* / *"Sad But True"*)
* **Sonic Goal:** Experience the exact historical evolution of James Hetfield's Mesa/Boogie Mark-series rhythm tone across two landmark eras:
* **Guitar Selection:** [[Guitars & Pickups#2. Epiphone Matt Heafy Les Paul Custom Origins 7-String|Epiphone Matt Heafy 7]] in **Voice 1 (Modern Active Ceramic)** (mirrors Hetfield's active EMG 81), or Schecter Reaper-6 (Crunch Lab; tune to **D Standard** for *"Sad But True"*).
* **Era 1 — 1986 *"Master of Puppets"* (Pure Unboosted Mark IIC+ Surgical Thrash):**
  * **Amp Channel:** **Mesa Mark VII — Channel 3 (`Mark IIC+ Mode`)**.
  * **Pre-Gain Controls:** `Gain: 7.5` *(Higher amp gain because no overdrive pedal was used in 1986)* | **`Bass: 1.5`** | `Mid: 3.5` | **`Treble: 7.5`** | `Presence: 6.0`.
  * **Front Pedals:** **NO TS808 / NO SD-1** (Use `EQ-200 Ch A` sub-cut `<60Hz` only if playing the 7-string to protect low-B).
  * **5-Band Graphic EQ ("Extreme 1986 Fleming Rasmussen Scoop"):** `80Hz: +85% (Top Line)` | `240Hz: 0% (Center)` | **`750Hz: -95% (Bottomed Out!)`** | `2200Hz: +50%` | `6600Hz: +45%`.
* **Era 2 — 1991 *Black Album* (Bob Rock Thick Wall of Sound):**
  * **Amp Channel:** **Mesa Mark VII — Channel 3 (`Mark IV Mode`)**.
  * **Pre-Gain Controls:** `Gain: 6.0` | `Bass: 2.5` | `Mid: 4.5` | `Treble: 6.5` | `Presence: 5.5`.
  * **Front Boost:** **Ibanez TS808** (`Level: 10`, `Drive: 0`, `Tone: 5.0`) for polished low-end discipline.
  * **5-Band Graphic EQ ("Less Scooped Producer V"):** `80Hz: +70%` | `240Hz: +20%` *(adds heavy chest weight)* | `750Hz: -65%` | `2200Hz: +45%` | `6600Hz: +35%`.
* **Cabinet IR:** **`York Audio MES 412 OS-V2` (`Mix JP` in Slot 8)**.

### 7. Tool – Adam Jones (*"Schism"*, *"Jambi"*, *"Lateralus"*, *"Forty Six & 2"*)
* **Sonic Goal:** Dry, chesty, ultra-percussive Drop-D midrange growl (originally a 1979 Marshall Super Bass + Diezel VH4 Blueface + Mesa Rectifier tri-amp blend). Unlike Metallica or Dream Theater, **Adam Jones does NOT scoop his mids** and uses **zero wet reverb** on rhythm tracks.
* **Guitar Selection & Tuning:**
  * [[Guitars & Pickups#3. Schecter Reaper-6 FR|Schecter Reaper-6]] in **Drop D (`D - A - D - G - B - E`)** on the **DiMarzio Crunch Lab** bridge pickup (ceramic magnet closely mirrors Adam's Seymour Duncan SH-6 Distortion / DDL).
* **Amplifier Option A — Mesa/Boogie Mark VII (Channel 2 Crunch / Channel 3 Mark VII):**
  * **Channel:** **Channel 2 (`Crunch Mode`)** (`Gain: 7.5`) or **Channel 3 (`Mark VII Mode`)** (`Gain: 5.5`).
  * **Controls:** `Bass: 2.5` | **`Mid: 7.5`** | `Treble: 6.5` | `Presence: 6.0`.
  * **5-Band Graphic EQ ("Flat-Mid Growl"):** `80Hz: +60%` | `240Hz: +20%` | **`750Hz: -10% (Keep near center!)`** | `2200Hz: +45%` | `6600Hz: +15%`.
* **Amplifier Option B — Marshall JVM 410C (Super Bass / Diezel Blend Emulation):**
  * **Channel:** **Crunch (`Red Mode`)** or **OD2 (`Green Mode`)** -> `Gain: 5.5` | `Bass: 6.5` | **`Mid: 7.5`** | `Treble: 6.0` | `Presence: 6.5` | `Resonance: 7.0`.
  * **FX Loop Sculpt:** **Boss EQ-200 Channel B** (`60Hz: +3dB`, `120Hz: +4dB`, `400Hz: -4dB`, `1.6kHz: +3dB`, `3.2kHz: +3dB`).
* **Clean Interludes (*"Schism"* / *"Lateralus"*):** Do **NOT** switch to a pristine clean channel—simply **roll the guitar Volume knob down to `4.0`** on your high-gain rhythm channel and engage the **TC HyperGravity** (`Spectra`, `Attack: 2:00`, `Blend: 55%`) for warm, woody, edge-of-breakup percussive hammer-ons!
